## Why we love football
The majority of the Code Labs team are football fans. We love to watch football, play football, and have kids that play football.

We are strong believers in the power of sports to bring people together, teach valuable life skills, and promote a healthy lifestyle. That's why we are proud to sponsor a local junior football team, **BP p15-5** (Brommapojkarna), which is one of the largest football clubs in Sweden.

Our own experience when working with others has shown us that teamwork, communication, and perseverance are essential for success. These are the same values that football tries to teach young players, and we want to support them in their journey to become not only better athletes but also better individuals.
